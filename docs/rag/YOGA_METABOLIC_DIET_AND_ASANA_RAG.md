## Chunk 1: Anthropometric Classification and BMI Risk Calibration (Global & Asian-Pacific Cutoffs)

Body Mass Index (BMI) is calculated from body mass in kilograms and stature in meters:

$$\text{BMI} = \frac{\text{Weight (kg)}}{\left(\text{Height (cm)} / 100\right)^2}$$

Clinical risk assessment must distinguish between Global WHO cutoffs and South Asian / Asian-Pacific thresholds due to elevated visceral adiposity risk at lower absolute BMI in Asian phenotypes:

| Classification Tier | Global WHO Cutoff ($\text{kg/m}^2$) | WHO Asia-Pacific / ICMR-NIN Cutoff ($\text{kg/m}^2$) | Primary Clinical & Yogic Programming Guidance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Underweight** | $< 18.5$ | $< 18.5$ | Controlled $300\text{--}500\text{ kcal/day}$ whole-food caloric surplus; avoid prolonged high-volume fasting cardio; emphasize isometric strength postures, digestive *Agni* stimulation, and restorative parasympathetic recovery. |
| **Healthy / Normal** | $18.5\text{--}24.9$ | $18.5\text{--}22.9$ | Isocaloric maintenance or goal-directed body recomposition ($\pm 200\text{ kcal/day}$); balanced Hatha + Vinyasa + Pranayama integration. |
| **Overweight** | $25.0\text{--}29.9$ | $23.0\text{--}24.9$ | Moderate $350\text{--}500\text{ kcal/day}$ deficit; dynamic metabolic sequences (*Surya Namaskar*, Warrior flows, Chair Pose) paired with high-fiber, protein-preserving meals and joint-safe alignment. |
| **Obese / High Cardiometabolic Risk** | $\ge 30.0$ | $\ge 25.0$ | Conservative $400\text{--}600\text{ kcal/day}$ deficit (never crashing below safety floors); low-impact joint-friendly dynamic sequences (chair/wall modifications), stress-cortisol control, and waist-circumference monitoring ($\ge 90\text{ cm}$ men, $\ge 80\text{ cm}$ women signals elevated visceral risk). |

## Chunk 2: Predictive Energy Equations (Mifflin-St Jeor BMR and TDEE Multipliers)

Resting energy expenditure is modeled via the validated **Mifflin-St Jeor (1990)** equation, carrying approximately $\pm 10\%$ individual variance:

- **Male BMR**: $\text{BMR} = (10 \times \text{weight}_{\text{kg}}) + (6.25 \times \text{height}_{\text{cm}}) - (5 \times \text{age}) + 5$
- **Female BMR**: $\text{BMR} = (10 \times \text{weight}_{\text{kg}}) + (6.25 \times \text{height}_{\text{cm}}) - (5 \times \text{age}) - 161$

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) scales BMR by physical activity multipliers:

| Activity Level | Multiplier | Operational Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sedentary** | $1.20$ | Desk-centric work with minimal structured physical movement. |
| **Lightly Active** | $1.375$ | Light mobility, gentle Hatha yoga, or walking 1--3 days/week. |
| **Moderately Active** | $1.55$ | Structured Vinyasa/Power yoga or moderate aerobic/strength training 3--5 days/week. |
| **Very Active** | $1.725$ | Vigorous athletic training, high-intensity *Surya Namaskar* + strength sessions 6--7 days/week. |

## Chunk 3: Weight Loss Caloric Deficit, Protein Sparing, and Metabolic Safety Floors

Sustainable adipose tissue loss without excessive lean muscle catabolism or metabolic downregulation requires a measured energy deficit:

1. **Recommended Caloric Deficit**:
   - **Moderate Clinical Guideline (ICMR-NIN / ACSM)**: $350\text{--}500\text{ kcal/day}$ below TDEE (roughly $15\%\text{--}20\%$ reduction), yielding approximately $0.35\text{--}0.5\text{ kg}$ fat loss per week.
   - **Higher BMI Ceiling**: Up to $500\text{--}650\text{ kcal/day}$ deficit only when TDEE is sufficiently elevated.
2. **Minimum Daily Energy Safety Floors**:
   - Never prescribe unsupervised caloric intakes below **$1,200\text{ kcal/day}$ (females)** or **$1,500\text{ kcal/day}$ (males)** to protect resting thyroid conversion, bone mineral density, micronutrients, and Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (NEAT).
3. **Protein-Sparing & Satiety Architecture**:
   - Maintain elevated protein intake during a caloric deficit: **$1.2\text{--}1.6\text{ g/kg}$** of body weight (higher $1.6\text{--}2.0\text{ g/kg}$ if resistance/power-yoga load is high; minimum ICMR-NIN RDA floor is $0.83\text{--}1.0\text{ g/kg}$).
   - High dietary fiber (**$28\text{--}38\text{ g/day}$**) from whole vegetables, salads, legumes, coarse millets (*ragi*, *jowar*, *bajra*, oats), and whole fruits delays gastric emptying and stimulates endogenous GLP-1 and PYY satiety hormones.
   - Follow the Harvard / ICMR plate allocation: fill **50% of lunch and dinner plates with non-starchy fibrous vegetables**, **25% with whole complex grains/millets**, and **25% with lean high-protein sources** (moong/masoor dal, paneer, tofu, sprouted legumes, curd/yogurt, eggs, fish/chicken where non-vegetarian), limiting refined sugars, sweetened beverages, and ultra-processed oils.

## Chunk 4: Healthy Weight Gain, Vegetarian Caloric Density, and Anabolic Nutrition

Achieving healthy lean mass gain (especially for underweight practitioners with BMI $< 18.5$ or vegetarian diets high in bulky fiber) must overcome early gastric satiety:

1. **Controlled Caloric Surplus**:
   - Add **$300\text{--}450\text{ kcal/day}$** ($+10\%\text{--}15\%$) above TDEE. Excess surpluses beyond $+700\text{ kcal/day}$ preferentially deposit visceral fat because skeletal muscle protein synthesis operates at a finite physiological ceiling.
2. **Macronutrient & Complete Amino Acid Strategy**:
   - **Protein**: Target **$1.5\text{--}1.8\text{ g/kg/day}$** distributed evenly across 4 meals/snacks ($25\text{--}35\text{ g}$ per feeding).
   - **Cereal + Legume Complementarity (ICMR-NIN 3:1 Ratio)**: Pair legumes/lentils (rich in lysine, lower in methionine) with whole grains/millets/brown rice (rich in methionine) such as rajma-rice, dal-quinoa/roti, chana-poha, or khichdi to form complete essential amino acid profiles.
3. **Caloric Density Engineering Without Empty Processed Sugars**:
   - Add nutrient-dense whole fats ($9\text{ kcal/g}$): $1\text{--}2\text{ tsp}$ cold-pressed mustard/olive oil or cow ghee on cooked dals/rotis, soaked almonds, walnuts, pumpkin/flax/chia seeds, and avocado/peanut butter.
   - **Semi-Solid Nutrition (Liquid Meal Bypass)**: Whole-food high-calorie smoothies (milk or soy milk + banana + roasted *sattu* or soaked oats + $2\text{ tbsp}$ peanut/almond butter + dates/cardamom) deliver $450\text{--}600\text{ kcal}$ cleanly without triggering premature gastric stretch satiety.
   - **Fiber Timing**: Consume bulky raw vegetable salads *after* or separate from primary dense meals during weight gain so low-calorie fiber does not prematurely shut down appetite before protein targets are met.
   - Pair caloric surplus with **isometric weight-bearing yoga holds** (Plank/*Phalakasana*, Warrior I & II, Chair Pose, Bridge Pose, Locust/Cobra) to signal muscle hypertrophy rather than visceral fat storage.

## Chunk 5: Compendium of Physical Activities (METs) and Yoga Energy Expenditure

Standard caloric burn calculation using Ainsworth et al. Compendium MET definitions ($1\text{ MET} = 3.5\text{ mL O}_2/\text{kg/min}$):

$$\text{Calories/min (kcal/min)} = \frac{\text{MET} \times \text{Weight (kg)} \times 3.5}{200}$$

| Yoga Practice Modality | Compendium MET Value | Approx. Burn (70 kg Adult, 30 min) | Metabolic & Clinical Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Restorative / Gentle Hatha & Pranayama** | $2.3\text{--}2.5$ METs | $\approx 84\text{--}92\text{ kcal}$ | Activates parasympathetic vagal tone, lowers serum/salivary cortisol, stabilizes appetite hormones and sleep. |
| **Vinyasa Flow (Moderate)** | $2.7\text{--}3.3$ METs | $\approx 99\text{--}121\text{ kcal}$ | Synchronizes rhythmic breathing with joint mobility, glucose uptake, and core endurance. |
| **Surya Namaskar (Dynamic Sun Salutation)** | $3.5\text{--}5.3$ METs | $\approx 129\text{--}195\text{ kcal}$ | Operates inside moderate aerobic zone ($60\%\text{--}70\%$ max HR); improves lipid profile, $\text{VO}_2$, insulin sensitivity, and adipose reduction. |
| **Power Yoga / Isometric Strength** | $4.0$ METs | $\approx 147\text{ kcal}$ | High time-under-tension across quadriceps, gluteals, core, and scapular stabilizers for lean tissue retention during deficit or hypertrophy during surplus. |

## Chunk 6: Therapeutic Asana Mechanisms for Weight Loss, Digestion, Endocrine Balance & Safety

1. **Weight Loss & Metabolic Activation Sequences**:
   - **Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutations, 6--12 rounds)**: Continuous flexion-extension aerobic cycle engaging major muscle chains and elevating active energy expenditure. Contraindications: acute lumbar disc protrusion, uncontrolled hypertension, wrist carpal tunnel (modify onto fists/forearms).
   - **Utkatasana (Chair Pose) & Virabhadrasana I/II (Warrior I & II)**: Large lower-extremity glycolytic muscle recruitment (`quadriceps`, `gluteus maximus`), boosting peripheral GLUT4 glucose transporter activation and insulin sensitivity.
   - **Navasana (Boat Pose) & Phalakasana (Plank Pose)**: Concentric/isometric anterior abdominal core recruitment without lumbar flexion jerking; improves metabolic stability and trunk bracing.
   - **Ardha Matsyendrasana (Half Lord of the Fishes Twist) & Bhujangasana (Cobra Pose)**: Stimulate visceral compression/release and gentle cervical-thoracic extension around thyroid vasculature to support $T_3/T_4$ endocrine tone.
   - **Kapalbhati Pranayama (High-Frequency Exhalation)**: Transiently increases metabolic rate and respiratory core endurance. **Strict Contraindications**: Uncontrolled hypertension, cardiac ischemia, inguinal/umbilical/hiatal hernia, active asthma flare, pregnancy, glaucoma.

2. **Healthy Weight Gain, Digestive Fire (*Agni*) & Cortisol-Lowering Sequences**:
   - **Vajrasana (Thunderbolt / Diamond Kneeling Pose, 5--10 min post-meals)**: Redirects peripheral lower-limb blood pooling toward splanchnic mesenteric arteries supplying stomach and small intestine, improving gastric motility, enzymatic secretion, and nutrient absorption right after lunch/dinner. *Modification*: Place folded bolster/cushion beneath ankles/knees if knee/ankle stiffness is present; avoid in severe knee osteoarthritis.
   - **Pavanamuktasana (Wind-Relieving Pose)**: Unilateral & bilateral knee-to-chest holds expel trapped colonic gas, relieve bloating/dyspepsia, and normalize sluggish appetite cues. Contraindicated in hernia, recent abdominal surgery, pregnancy.
   - **Virabhadrasana II, Phalakasana, Setu Bandhasana (Bridge) & Malasana (Yogic Squat)**: Sustained 30--60s isometric mechanical tension stimulates myofibrillar protein retention when paired with adequate protein and caloric surplus.
   - **Paschimottanasana (Seated Forward Fold), Balasana (Child's Pose) & Savasana**: Downregulate sympathetic hyperarousal and elevated HPA-axis cortisol—essential for underweight/stressed "hard-gainers" whose chronic cortisol spikes fuel catabolic tissue breakdown, and equally crucial in weight loss to prevent stress-driven visceral fat storage. Contraindicated (deep unassisted forward folds) in acute lumbar disc herniation or sciatica (bend knees generously or support torso on bolsters).
   - **Nadi Shodhana (Alternate Nostril) & Bhramari Pranayama**: Proven vagal stimulants reducing resting heart rate, stress-eating impulses, and nocturnal insomnia.

## Chunk 7: Daily Circadian Hydration, Breathwork, and Sleep Integration

1. **Hydration Standard (EFSA / ICMR)**:
   - Baseline fluid target scales with body weight and caloric/sweat demand: approximately **$33\text{--}35\text{ mL/kg/day}$** ($2.3\text{--}3.5\text{ L/day}$ baseline), adjusted upward by $+400\text{--}500\text{ mL}$ on dynamic *Surya Namaskar* / Power Vinyasa days.
   - For **Weight Loss**: Drink $300\text{--}400\text{ mL}$ warm/plain water $20\text{--}30$ minutes before main meals to aid gastric satiety and avoid liquid-calorie beverages.
   - For **Weight Gain**: Avoid excessive plain water immediately *before* or *during* meals (prevents premature stomach filling); hydrate between meals and prioritize nutrient-dense milk/smoothies post-practice.
2. **Sleep & Recovery**:
   - Maintain **$7.0\text{--}8.5\text{ hours}$** of consistent nocturnal sleep. Sleep restriction below $6\text{ hours}$ elevates ghrelin (hunger hormone), lowers leptin, spikes evening cortisol, and shifts weight loss away from fat toward lean muscle loss.
